## Dutch Funding Landscape

*Researched October 2026. Amounts and eligibility checked against each fund's own pages.*

### Best Current Opportunities

<div class="featured-grant" markdown="1">
**Mondriaan Artist Basic — €50,000** over 2–4 years. Rolling, apply any time; decision in ~3 months.
</div>

<div class="featured-grant" markdown="1">
**Mondriaan Artist Project — up to €16,710** (€2,785/month × 6 months). Rolling, apply any time.
</div>

Basic and Project cannot fund the same costs — choose one per plan.

### National Funds

**Mondriaan Fund** — Primary national fund for visual arts
- [mondriaanfonds.nl — grants overview](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/)
- Budget: ~€46 million annual state funding; €50.3 million in grants awarded in 2025
- 2025–2028 policy: "Beyond the Centre, Beyond the Norm"
- All individual schemes are rolling: no rounds, no retroactive funding

| Grant | Amount | Duration | For |
|-------|--------|----------|-----|
| [Artist Basic](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/artist-basic/) | €50,000 | 2-4 years | Oeuvre development |
| [Artist Project](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/artist-project/) | €2,785/month (max €16,710), or budget-based up to 12 months | Up to 6 / 12 months | Research/working period |
| [International Art Presentation](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/international-art-presentation/) | Per cost type (lodging up to €1,000 p.p.) | — | Showing work abroad; needs a foreign host invitation |
| [Artist Start](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/artist-start/) | €24,900 | 12 months | 1–4 years of practice only — *no longer eligible* |

**Requirements:**
- Artist Basic: minimum 4 years professional practice (counted from BA diploma); taxable income ≤ €55,000/year in each of the previous 2 years; once per 4 years; 12-month wait after a rejection on quality
- Artist Project: minimum 1 year professional practice; at most 24 months of support in any 48
- Work must contribute to contemporary visual art in NL; non-Dutch nationals add a population-register extract (< 1 year old)

### Municipal Funds (The Hague)

**Stroom Den Haag** — Local arts support
- [stroom.nl — financial support](https://www.stroom.nl/stroom-voor-kunstenaars/financiele-ondersteuning)
- Apply via the online portal; requires an up-to-date profile in Stroom's Hague artists register
- Max €20,000 per applicant per calendar year; max 2 PRO applications per year
- Projects may start no earlier than 8 weeks after the round deadline

| Grant | Max Amount | Notes |
|-------|------------|-------|
| PRO Art Projects | €10,000 | ≥30% co-financing above €5,000 |
| PRO Art Programme | €10,000 | For organisations and independent curators |
| PRO Research | €5,000 | Research projects |
| PRO Invest | €4,000 | Within 5 years of graduating — *no longer eligible* |
| SPOT | €1,500 NL / €2,000 abroad | Closed for 2026 |
| OBJECT | — | Studio costs; closed for 2026 |

**Also offers:** Studio program, documentation services

### Awards

**Koninklijke Prijs voor Vrije Schilderkunst**
- 3 × €9,000 prizes (tax-free), plus a public prize
- Open application; under 35 on 1 January of the prize year
- 2027 edition closed 31 August 2026; age limit rules out 2028 onward
- [paleisamsterdam.nl — Royal Award](https://www.paleisamsterdam.nl/en/exhibition-royal-award/)

**Not open to application:**
- [Prix de Rome](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/activities/prix-de-rome/) — €60,000 (2025); no age limit since 2023, but candidates are scouted by the jury
- [Cultuurfonds Talentprijs Charlotte Köhler](https://www.cultuurfonds.nl/cultuurfonds-talentprijs-charlotte-k%C3%B6hler) — €20,000; under 35, jury-selected

## Application Checklist

### Before Applying

- [ ] Check eligibility (age, location, career stage)
- [ ] Note deadline and required format
- [ ] Review selection criteria
- [ ] Check budget limits and co-financing requirements
- [ ] Gather required attachments (portfolio, CV, references)

### Application Contents

- [ ] Completed application form
- [ ] Project description (within word limit)
- [ ] Artist statement
- [ ] CV (formatted per requirements)
- [ ] Portfolio (10-20 images, specified resolution)
- [ ] Budget breakdown
- [ ] Timeline
- [ ] Letters of support/confirmation (if required)

### Portfolio Image Specifications

Typical requirements:
- JPEG format
- 72-300 dpi
- Max 5MB per image
- Filename: `LastName_Title_Year_Number.jpg`
- Include image list with: title, year, medium, dimensions

### After Submitting

- [ ] Save confirmation email/number
- [ ] Note expected response date
- [ ] Calendar reminder for result
- [ ] If rejected: request feedback (if offered)
