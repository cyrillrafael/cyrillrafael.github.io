## Opportunity Profiles

Requirements as stated in each call; limits quoted exactly.

### International

#### Pollock-Krasner Foundation Grant

- **Gives:** up to $50,000 for one year, unrestricted; most grants around $35,000
- **When:** rolling; decision in 9–12 months; 2-year wait after a decline
- **Eligibility:** painters and other visual artists, any age, worldwide; at least 10 exhibitions in the past 10 years at professional venues
- **Nationality:** citizens or residents of US-sanctioned countries contact the foundation before applying
- **Submit:** one-page cover letter; CV with solo and group shows in separate sections; one-page statement; image list; exactly 10 JPEGs, longest side 2100 px, at most 2 detail or installation views, all from the last 10 years; 3 reference contacts
- **How:** online, English, no fee; [pkf.org/how-to-apply](https://www.pkf.org/how-to-apply/)

#### Art Explora × Cité internationale des arts {#art-explora}

- **Gives:** ~50 m² live-work studio in Paris, €1,000/month, €3,000 production grant, return trip; 3 or 6 months
- **When:** 30 October 2026, 23:59 Paris time; results January 2027 (session 1, March–August 2027) or June 2027 (session 2, September 2027 – February 2028)
- **Eligibility:** all nationalities, no age limit; 5+ years of practice; international presentation of work
- **Submit:** project description, "2 pages maximum", including the reason for France; portfolio, "15 pages maximum"; CV, "2 pages maximum"; audience activities summary, "1,500 characters maximum (including spaces)"; English
- **If selected:** open studios in February and June (mandatory); one workshop or intervention
- **How:** free online form, not editable once finalised; [call PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6107fc8664b5410e82635537/6aad537c9404c9c3a634bbb4_Opencall.pdf), [apply](https://artexplora.plateformecandidature.com/)

#### Kone Foundation Saari Residence

- **Gives:** 2 months in Mynämäki, Finland, with apartment and workspace; 2027-cycle grants of €2,700, €3,200 or €3,800 a month by experience; travel support
- **When:** 1–31 March 2027, closing 16:00 Finnish time; decisions mid-June 2027; residencies in 2028
- **Eligibility:** "all disciplines and nationalities"
- **How:** [koneensaatio.fi](https://koneensaatio.fi/en/saari-residence/residency-applicants)

### Netherlands

#### Mondriaan Fund

- **Eligibility:** embedded in Dutch professional practice; non-Dutch nationals attach a BRP extract under one year old; [terms](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/terms-and-conditions/)
- **When:** rolling; about 3 months to decision; no retroactive funding

**[Artist Basic](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/artist-basic/): €50,000 over 2–4 years**
- ≥4 years of professional practice since the BA; taxable income ≤ €55,000 in each of the previous 2 years; at most once every 4 years
- Explanation of work and cultural entrepreneurship, "max. 3 pages"
- Curriculum vitae, "3 pages maximum"; working plan, "2 pages maximum"
- Visual documentation, "max. 20 pages", one image per page; audio/video up to 15 minutes in total; documentation list, "max. 2 pages"
- Uploads max 500 MB; tax returns for the last 2 years; recent bank statement

**[Artist Project](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/artist-project/): €2,785/month for up to 6 months (max €16,710)**
- Or budget-based for up to 12 months with ≥10% own contribution; at most 24 months of support in any 48
- Covers "a stay at a residency in the Netherlands or abroad"
- Not combinable with Artist Basic for the same costs

**Other rolling grants**
- [International Art Presentation](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/international-art-presentation/): travel, transport, lodging up to €1,000, 50% of new production; recognised foreign institutions, not commercial galleries
- [Voucher International](https://www.mondriaanfonds.nl/en/apply-for-a-grant/grants/voucher-international/): up to €2,500 travel for an invited talk abroad; 4 months to 5 weeks ahead; max twice in 12 months

#### Stroom Den Haag

- **Gives:** [PRO Art Projects](https://stroom.nl/en/stroom-for-artists/financial-support/pro-art-projects) up to €10,000, ≥30% co-financing above €5,000; [PRO Research](https://stroom.nl/en/stroom-for-artists/financial-support/pro-research) up to €5,000
- **When:** 2026 rounds 4 Jan, 22 Feb, 12 Apr, 31 May, 6 Sep, 25 Oct; 2027 not published; projects start ≥8 weeks after the round
- **Eligibility:** living in The Hague; up-to-date profile in Stroom's artist register
- **Limits:** €20,000 per applicant per calendar year; 2 PRO applications per year; moves during a project must be reported
- **How:** online portal; [financial support](https://www.stroom.nl/stroom-voor-kunstenaars/financiele-ondersteuning)

#### Stokroos

- **[Seed](https://stokroos.nl/grant-rules/seed/):** €5,000; graduates of 2018–2022
- **Residency Nairs:** 3 months plus €7,000
- **Makersinitiatieven:** €25,000 or €40,000; practising since 2020 or earlier, with a track record
- **Eligibility:** at least one year living and working in the Kingdom of the Netherlands
- **When:** 2026 windows closed; 2027 expected: Seed May–June and September–October, Makersinitiatieven late July; [stokroos.nl](https://www.stokroos.nl/)

#### Culture Moves Europe

- **Gives:** €85/day for 7–60 days plus €400–800 travel, for a stay in another Creative Europe country
- **When:** monthly, 31 October 2026 to 30 April 2027, none in December
- **Eligibility:** legal residence in a Creative Europe country
- **How:** [call details](https://on-the-move.org/news/culture-moves-europe-call-individual-mobility-2026-2027)

#### Jan van Eyck Academie 2028

- **Gives:** 11 months in Maastricht, 15 January – 15 December 2028; €1,500/month with rent deducted; €2,000/year work budget; studio apartment; visa support
- **When:** 1 December 2026, 23:59 CET; selection mid-May 2027
- **Costs:** application €65 excl. VAT; enrolment €2,750 excl. VAT if selected
- **Eligibility:** residence in Maastricht during the programme; Rijksakademie and De Ateliers alumni excluded
- **Submit:** statement 250 words / 1,400 characters; motivation 500 words / 3,150 characters; proposal 500 words / 3,150 characters; or one video/audio file, max 8 min. One PDF, max 10 MB: max 25 pages of work from the last 5 years plus a 2-page CV, landscape, 1–2 works per page, captioned. Links live for 3 months.
- **How:** Dapple account, English; [call](https://janvaneyck.nl/calendar/apply-now-jve-residency-2028), [apply](https://app.dapplehq.com/o/jan-van-eyck-academie)

#### Rijksakademie Residency 2028

- **Gives:** ~€19,800/year stipend and €2,500/year work budget (2026 figures), less a €1,875/year participation fee; one year, optional second
- **When:** opens 1 January 2027; previous deadline 1 February
- **Eligibility:** all nationalities, no age limit (most applicants 26–35); half the places for Dutch nationals or those who lived and worked in NL ≥2 years; max 3 applications per lifetime; JvE and De Ateliers alumni excluded
- **Costs:** €60 application fee
- **Submit:** round 1 portfolio only; later rounds texts, motivation, intro video, interview; upload limits not yet published
- **How:** [rijksakademie.nl/apply](https://www.rijksakademie.nl/en/residency-apply/apply)

#### De Ateliers

- **Gives:** two years in Amsterdam, 2027–2029; ~€18,000/year (site quotes €17,916 and €18,804); €2,100/year production; €3,200 fee
- **When:** opens 1 December 2026; previous deadline 15 January, 23:59 CET
- **Eligibility:** participants "usually 20 to 30"; Rijksakademie and JvE alumni excluded
- **Submit:** €65 fee; online form in four parts (personal data, portfolio and statement, motivation, submit); English
- **How:** [de-ateliers.nl/apply](https://de-ateliers.nl/en/apply/)

### China

#### Sixi International AIR, Nanjing

- **Gives:** visa fees, return flights, insurance, apartment or loft studio, translation, materials allowance; about 3 months, March–May or September–November 2027
- **When:** 15 November 2026 (GMT+8); results by mid-February 2027
- **Eligibility:** any nationality, 18+; Mandarin or English; proposal addresses one of two set themes
- **Submit:** CV with photo; portfolio; proposal (motivation, plan, concept, materials, preferred period); English, Chinese if possible; no stated limits
- **If selected:** talk, workshop, open studio; a presentable outcome; a selection of works donated
- **How:** one PDF or ZIP to info@siximuseum.com, subject "Residency Application"; no fee; possible video interview; [siximuseum.com](https://siximuseum.com/en/residency/)

#### A4 International Residency, Chengdu

- **Gives:** fellowship: studio, housing, economy flight or second-class train within a budget, ¥10,000 production; light track: studio and housing; at least 8 weeks, July–October 2028
- **When:** 31 December 2026 (autumn 2028 session)
- **Eligibility:** no nationality limit stated
- **Submit:** all materials in English and Chinese; photo; portfolio; application form (.doc); CV; proposal covering community or commercial engagement
- **If selected:** 1–2 public activities, open studio, field trip, filming and interviews
- **How:** a4residency@a4am.cn, subject "A4 International Residency Application – [Name] – 2028 Autumn"; [call](https://www.a4artmuseum.com/en/a4-residencyartcenter/residing/latest-recruitment/)

## Application Checklist

### Before Applying

- [ ] Check eligibility (age, location, career stage)
- [ ] Note deadline and required format
- [ ] Review selection criteria
- [ ] Check budget limits and co-financing requirements
- [ ] Gather required attachments (portfolio, CV, references)

### Application Contents

- [ ] Completed application form
- [ ] Project description (within word limit)
- [ ] Artist statement
- [ ] CV (formatted per requirements)
- [ ] Portfolio (10-20 images, specified resolution)
- [ ] Budget breakdown
- [ ] Timeline
- [ ] Letters of support/confirmation (if required)

### Portfolio Image Specifications

Typical requirements:
- JPEG format
- 72-300 dpi
- Max 5MB per image
- Filename: `LastName_Title_Year_Number.jpg`
- Include image list with: title, year, medium, dimensions

### After Submitting

- [ ] Save confirmation email/number
- [ ] Note expected response date
- [ ] Calendar reminder for result
- [ ] If rejected: request feedback (if offered)
